Sign in with your Google account to track your own expenses. Everyone gets their own private page — no one else can see your data.

This app hasn't been connected to a Firebase project yet. Open this file, find FIREBASE_CONFIG near the top of the script, and paste in your Firebase web app config. Also make sure Google sign-in is enabled under Authentication → Sign-in method, and that a Firestore database exists with a rule allowing each user to read/write only /users/{their own uid}.
